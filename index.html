Professional Summary

I am a versatile IT Support Engineer, Data Analyst, and aspiring Data Scientist with over three years of experience supporting ICT systems, analyzing data, and developing technology-driven solutions. I specialize in Python, SQL, PostgreSQL, Excel, and R, transforming complex datasets into actionable insights that inform strategic decision-making.

In addition to analytics, I bring strong software development expertise in web design and development using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, and WordPress, along with practical knowledge of APIs and system integration.

I have successfully combined my technical knowledge with excellent communication and leadership skills — training healthcare professionals, mentoring students, and fostering a culture of problem-solving and digital innovation.

Passionate about digital transformation, I strive to leverage data, software engineering, and ICT infrastructure to deliver impactful, scalable, and sustainable solutions that drive organizational growth and efficiency.
